# Daniel Azubuine Portfolio

This portfolio presents Daniel Azubuine's creative practice as one authored body of work expressed through music and software. Daniel is the founder and creator of Solfa Streams.

## Key Facts

**Solfa Streams**:
A product founded and created by Daniel Azubuine. Reference Daniel as its founder or creator when credentialing him in a software or product context.
_Avoid_: Co-founder, contributor

## Language

**Creative Practice**:
Daniel's unified body of work, encompassing both music and software without treating either as a separate identity.
_Avoid_: Dual career, two portfolios, split identity

**Music Mode**:
The cinematic and emotionally immersive presentation of Daniel's compositions, performances, recordings, and music credits.
_Avoid_: Music side, musician portfolio

**Software Mode**:
The precise and evidence-led presentation of Daniel's software projects, engineering craft, and technical writing.
_Avoid_: Tech side, developer portfolio

**Unified Experience**:
The site-wide experience in which Music Mode and Software Mode retain distinct visual grammars while sharing one identity and design system. The homepage is its flagship single-page journey, while deeper pages provide complete project and catalog detail.
_Avoid_: Combined website, portfolio hub, homepage redesign

**Conversation**:
The portfolio's primary conversion: a serious inquiry about a software role, product collaboration, music commission, performance, or creative partnership.
_Avoid_: Lead, conversion event, contact-form submission

**Public Experience**:
The visitor-facing expression of the Creative Practice, where cinematic motion, advanced graphics, sound, and interaction may be used progressively to create impact.
_Avoid_: Frontend, marketing site

**Studio Console**:
The private administrative workspace for managing portfolio content. It shares the design system but prioritizes speed, clarity, accessibility, and dependable form behavior over spectacle.
_Avoid_: Admin experience, public admin page

**Software Project**:
A structured account of a software outcome, including its problem, Daniel's role, process, implementation evidence, result, media, and relevant links.
_Avoid_: Project card, external project link, work item

**Composition**:
A musical work presented with its artistic context, instrumentation, recordings, score or performance availability, credits, history, media, and related works.
_Avoid_: Catalog item, song card, music project

**Work Story**:
The editorial presentation of a Software Project or Composition at enough depth for a visitor to understand its intent, craft, and outcome.
_Avoid_: Detail page, CMS entry, portfolio item

**Sonic Layer**:
The optional audio-reactive dimension of the Public Experience. It begins silent, requires deliberate activation, and enhances graphics and motion without being necessary to understand or use the site.
_Avoid_: Background music, autoplay soundtrack, audio mode

**Silent Experience**:
The complete Public Experience when audio is unavailable or disabled, including equivalent information, interaction feedback, and visual meaning.
_Avoid_: Fallback site, degraded mode

**Resonance Field**:
The site-wide art direction: a luminous generative field that can read as musical resonance or computational structure, changing character between Music Mode and Software Mode while remaining one visual system.
_Avoid_: Audio visualizer, particle background, tech aesthetic
