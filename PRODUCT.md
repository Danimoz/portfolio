# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The portfolio serves design-conscious software hiring teams and collaborators, music commissioners and directors, performers and listeners, and peers exploring Daniel Azubuine's work. They need to understand his point of view quickly, reach credible evidence in their own discipline, and find a clear path to a serious conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Daniel's Creative Practice as one authored body of work expressed through Music Mode and Software Mode. Success means a visitor can recognize the relationship between those modes, evaluate relevant Work Stories without friction, and start a Conversation about a role, commission, performance, product, or creative partnership.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, exacting, resonant.

## Anti-references

- A visual copy of Eric Whitacre, Christopher Tin, Christopher Slaski, or Victor Williams
- A generic dark technology portfolio built from neon blobs, glass cards, dashboard chrome, and decorative grids
- A conventional classical-music template with ornamental typography and static catalog lists
- Forced code-and-music metaphors, symphony language, or notation used as decoration
- Spectacle that hides authentic work, delays navigation, autoplays sound, or excludes lower-powered devices

## Design Principles

1. **One practice, two modes.** Music and software keep distinct visual grammars while belonging to one continuous identity.
2. **Proof earns attention.** Real projects, compositions, recordings, decisions, and outcomes carry more weight than promotional claims.
3. **A Conversation is the destination.** Navigation, Work Stories, listening, and technical evidence support a clear invitation to connect.
4. **Advanced by invitation.** Sound, shaders, and cinematic motion enhance the experience progressively and never become entry requirements.
5. **Restraint protects impact.** The Resonance Field becomes most expressive at narrative transitions and quiet around content that needs careful reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across visitor-facing pages and the Studio Console. Provide complete keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic structure, a complete Silent Experience, reduced-motion behavior, touch-safe targets, and resilient layouts for zoom, small screens, reduced data, and lower-powered devices. Audio requires deliberate activation and remains independently mutable.
